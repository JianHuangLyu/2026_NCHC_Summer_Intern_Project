Skill_Name: Lymphoid_and_Hematopoietic_Architecture

Description:
This new conditional skill describes lymphoid and hematopoietic tissue architecture and visible cell-population morphology without assigning lineage, clonality, or diagnosis.

Task:
Evaluate lymphoid or hematopoietic organization, population composition, and spatial distribution.

Assess:
- Diffuse, nodular, follicular, interfollicular, sinusoidal, paracortical-like, or mixed organization
- Follicle-like structures, mantle-like zones, germinal-center-like areas, sinuses, cords, and marrow spaces when visible
- Small, intermediate, large, plasmacytoid, histiocytic, blast-like, or mixed cell populations
- Population monotony, heterogeneity, cohesion, crush artifact, apoptosis, necrosis, mitoses, and background material

Rules:
1. Apply Global_Rules.md.
2. Do not infer B-cell, T-cell, plasma-cell, myeloid, or other lineage without ancillary evidence.
3. Do not infer clonality, lymphoma, leukemia, reactive status, or subtype.
4. Use `-like` for follicular, blast, germinal-center, or zonal descriptions that cannot be confirmed.
5. Report crush artifact and limited tissue explicitly.

Output:
- Lymphoid_or_Hematopoietic_Tissue
- Architectural_Pattern
- Follicle_or_Zone_Morphology
- Predominant_Cell_Population_Morphology
- Population_Uniformity_and_Heterogeneity
- Sinus_Cord_or_Marrow_Space_Organization
- Background_and_Debris
- Assessability
- Limitations
