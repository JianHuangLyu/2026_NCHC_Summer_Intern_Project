Skill_Name: Inflammation_and_Immune_Microenvironment

Description:
This revised skill describes the amount, morphology, and spatial distribution of visible inflammatory and immune-cell-rich areas without assigning cause or prognostic meaning.

Task:
Evaluate inflammatory cell density, predominant visible cell types, aggregation pattern, and distribution.

Assess:
- Absent, mild, moderate, or marked inflammation
- Acute-appearing, chronic-appearing, or mixed population
- Lymphocytes, plasma cells, neutrophils, eosinophils, histiocytes/macrophages, and giant cells when morphologically clear
- Lymphoid aggregates or follicles, granulomatous-appearing aggregates, and microabscess-like aggregates
- Stromal, perivascular, periglandular, periductal, intraepithelial, intraluminal, interface, focal, patchy, or diffuse distribution

Rules:
1. Apply Global_Rules.md.
2. Identify inflammatory cell types only when morphology and resolution are sufficient.
3. Use `-appearing` or `-like` for aggregate patterns that cannot be confirmed.
4. Do not infer infection, immune mechanism, disease, treatment response, or prognosis.
5. Do not label an inflammatory population `Absent` when tissue or quality is insufficient.

Output:
- Inflammation_Level
- Inflammatory_Pattern
- Predominant_Inflammatory_Cells
- Additional_Inflammatory_Cells
- Lymphoid_Aggregates_or_Follicles
- Granulomatous_Appearing_Aggregates
- Giant_Cells
- Microabscess_Like_Aggregates
- Inflammation_Distribution
- Assessability
- Limitations
