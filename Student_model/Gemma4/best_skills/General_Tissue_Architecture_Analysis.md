Skill_Name: General_Tissue_Architecture_Analysis

Description:
This revised skill describes cross-organ spatial arrangement using lineage-neutral architectural patterns. Epithelial-specific patterns are delegated to a conditional module.

Task:
Identify dominant, secondary, and mixed architectural arrangements visible within the ROI.

Assess:
- Diffuse, sheet-like, nested, lobular, nodular, fascicular, storiform, trabecular or cord-like, single-file, palisading, perivascular, reticular, plexiform, sinusoidal, follicular, patternless or haphazard, zonal, biphasic, or multicompartmental arrangement
- Alternating hypercellular and hypocellular regions
- Structural fusion, organization, complexity, and preserved versus disrupted native architecture
- Pattern proportion and spatial distribution

Rules:
1. Apply Global_Rules.md.
2. Select multiple patterns only when each is independently visible.
3. Do not infer a complete lesion architecture from a small or partial ROI.
4. Do not use architectural patterns to name a disease or classify biological behavior.
5. Delegate papillary, cribriform, glandular, epithelial layering, and bridging to Epithelial_and_Glandular_Architecture when applicable.

Output:
- Dominant_Architectural_Pattern
- Secondary_Architectural_Patterns
- Pattern_Proportions
- Pattern_Distribution
- Cellular_Arrangement
- Cellularity_Distribution
- Structural_Fusion
- Structural_Complexity
- Architectural_Organization
- Preserved_or_Disrupted_Native_Architecture
- Assessability
- Limitations
