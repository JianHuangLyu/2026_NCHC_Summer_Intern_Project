Skill_Name: Cell_Death_Mitotic_Activity_and_Deposition_Analysis

Description:
This revised skill describes mitotic figures, cell-death morphology, debris, mineralization, pigments, extracellular deposits, and foreign material as independent observations.

Task:
Detect, localize, and cautiously quantify visible proliferative, degenerative, necrotic, and deposited material.

Assess:
- Typical and possible atypical mitotic figures; approximate frequency, counted area, unit, and hotspot status
- Geographic, coagulative-appearing, focal, central, single-cell, or indeterminate necrotic change
- Apoptotic bodies, karyorrhectic debris, ghost-cell-like areas, and cellular debris
- Fine, coarse, punctate, irregular, linear, branching, clustered, or diffuse calcification/mineralization
- Keratinous, mucin-like, proteinaceous, fibrin-like, amyloid-like, hemosiderin-like, melanin-like, bile-like, crystalline, cholesterol-cleft-like, or foreign material

Rules:
1. Apply Global_Rules.md.
2. Do not assign histological grade or interpret mitotic activity as proof of malignancy.
3. Record frequency only with the visible counted area and method; never extrapolate a small ROI to the whole specimen.
4. Avoid misclassifying apoptosis, pyknotic or crushed nuclei, stain deposits, folds, or foreign material.
5. Use `-like` or `-appearing` for material not confirmed by ancillary methods.

Output:
- Mitotic_Figures
- Approximate_Mitotic_Frequency
- Mitotic_Count_Area_and_Unit
- Hotspot_Status
- Possible_Atypical_Mitoses
- Necrosis_or_Cell_Death
- Necrosis_Extent
- Necrosis_Distribution
- Apoptotic_or_Karyorrhectic_Debris
- Cellular_Debris
- Calcification_or_Mineralization
- Calcification_Morphology
- Deposited_or_Pigmented_Material
- Foreign_Material
- Distribution
- Assessability
- Limitations
