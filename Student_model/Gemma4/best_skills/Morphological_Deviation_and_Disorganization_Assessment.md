Skill_Name: Morphological_Deviation_and_Disorganization_Assessment

Description:
This revised conditional skill assesses visible variation and disorganization only when an appropriate visible reference or internally comparable tissue is available. Its levels are not histological grades.

Task:
Describe cellular and architectural deviation with explicit reference context and supporting evidence.

Assess:
- Reference tissue availability and comparability
- Cellular crowding and size/shape variation
- Nuclear size, shape, contour, and chromatin variation
- Loss of orientation or polarity when the relevant structure normally has assessable orientation
- Architectural complexity, fusion, distortion, heterogeneity, and disorganization

Rules:
1. Apply Global_Rules.md.
2. Do not assign an abnormality level without adequate quality and an appropriate comparison context.
3. Use `Minimal`, `Mild`, `Moderate`, or `Marked morphological deviation`; never use these as low- or high-grade categories.
4. Evaluate cellular and architectural deviation separately.
5. Polarity is `N/A` for structures without an applicable directional organization.
6. List the visible evidence supporting every level.

Output:
- Reference_Context
- Reference_Adequacy
- Cellular_Morphological_Deviation
- Architectural_Morphological_Deviation
- Cellular_Crowding
- Nuclear_Variation
- Orientation_or_Polarity
- Architectural_Disorganization
- Supporting_Visible_Features
- Assessability
- Limitations
