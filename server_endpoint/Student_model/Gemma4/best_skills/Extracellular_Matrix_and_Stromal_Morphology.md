Skill_Name: Extracellular_Matrix_and_Stromal_Morphology

Description:
This revised skill describes extracellular matrix and stroma independently of epithelial context and without assigning invasion or a specific disease.

Task:
Evaluate matrix amount, type, density, organization, cellularity, and distribution.

Assess:
- Scant, moderate, or abundant matrix/stroma
- Loose, fibrous, collagenous, hyalinized, myxoid, myxocollagenous, chondroid-appearing, osteoid-like, basement-membrane-like, fibrinoid-like, mucin-like, or mixed matrix
- Collagen thickness and organization
- Fibrosis, sclerosis, edema, stromal cellularity, stromal cell shape, and regional heterogeneity

Rules:
1. Apply Global_Rules.md.
2. Use composition-confirming terms only when morphology is sufficient; otherwise use `-like`, `-appearing`, nonspecific, or indeterminate.
3. Do not use stromal response alone to infer invasion.
4. Avoid `desmoplastic reaction` unless independently established outside this non-diagnostic workflow; use `dense fibrotic stromal response`.
5. Separate matrix morphology from inflammatory and vascular findings handled by their own modules.

Output:
- Matrix_or_Stromal_Amount
- Matrix_or_Stromal_Type
- Matrix_Density
- Matrix_Organization
- Collagen_Morphology
- Fibrosis
- Sclerosis
- Edema
- Myxoid_Change
- Stromal_Cellularity
- Stromal_Cell_Morphology
- Regional_Heterogeneity
- Assessability
- Limitations
