Skill_Name: Muscle_Tissue_Morphology

Description:
This new conditional skill describes visible smooth- or skeletal-muscle-like tissue architecture and cellular morphology without confirming differentiation or diagnosing a lesion.

Task:
Evaluate muscle bundle organization, cell morphology, striation visibility, degeneration, necrosis, fibrosis, and inflammatory relationships.

Assess:
- Fascicles, bundles, fibers, cross-sectional versus longitudinal orientation, and interdigitation
- Elongated eosinophilic cells, cigar-shaped or peripheral nuclei, cross-striations when visible, and fiber-size variation
- Splitting, vacuolation, degeneration, necrosis, regeneration-like basophilia, fibrosis, fatty replacement, and inflammation

Rules:
1. Apply Global_Rules.md.
2. Use smooth-muscle-like or skeletal-muscle-like unless differentiation is unequivocally visible.
3. Do not infer myogenic tumour type, dystrophy, inflammatory myopathy, or cause.
4. Do not call regeneration or degeneration definitively when morphology is ambiguous.
5. Interpret fiber-size variation only when orientation and sampling permit comparison.

Output:
- Muscle_Component
- Muscle_Like_Type
- Bundle_or_Fiber_Organization
- Cellular_and_Nuclear_Morphology
- Striations
- Fiber_Size_Variation
- Degenerative_or_Necrotic_Change
- Fibrosis_or_Fatty_Replacement
- Inflammation_Distribution
- Assessability
- Limitations
