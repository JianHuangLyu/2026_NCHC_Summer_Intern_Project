Skill_Name: Bone_Cartilage_and_Osteoid_Morphology

Description:
This new conditional skill describes bone, cartilage, osteoid-like, chondroid-appearing, and mineralized matrices without diagnosing a bone or cartilage lesion.

Task:
Evaluate matrix type, maturation, cellular relationship, mineralization, and visible interface with native bone or soft tissue.

Assess:
- Lamellar, woven-appearing, trabecular, cortical, or indeterminate bone
- Osteoid-like, chondroid-appearing, myxochondroid-appearing, fibrous, or mineralized matrix
- Lacunae, osteoblast-like rimming, osteoclast-like giant cells, chondrocyte-like cells, and zonation when visible
- Matrix amount, distribution, maturation gradient, calcification, necrosis, fracture-like change, and interface

Rules:
1. Apply Global_Rules.md.
2. Use `-like` or `-appearing` when matrix identity or cell lineage is not confirmed.
3. Do not infer neoplastic production, malignancy, tumour type, or grade from osteoid- or cartilage-like material.
4. Distinguish mineralization from stain precipitate or foreign material when possible.
5. Do not infer a complete maturation pattern from a partial ROI.

Output:
- Bone_or_Cartilage_Component
- Matrix_Type
- Matrix_Amount_and_Distribution
- Bone_or_Cartilage_Organization
- Cell_Matrix_Relationship
- Rimming_Lacunae_or_Giant_Cells
- Maturation_or_Zonation
- Mineralization
- Interface
- Assessability
- Limitations
