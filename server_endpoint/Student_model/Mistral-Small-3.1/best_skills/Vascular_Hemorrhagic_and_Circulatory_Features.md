Skill_Name: Vascular_Hemorrhagic_and_Circulatory_Features

Description:
This new skill describes visible vessels, vascular-like channels, hemorrhage, congestion, extravasated erythrocytes, and thrombus- or fibrin-like material.

Task:
Evaluate vascular morphology and circulatory findings without inferring vascular differentiation or a vascular lesion.

Assess:
- Vessel or channel presence, density, caliber, wall thickness, and distribution
- Thin-walled, thick-walled, branching, arborizing, slit-like, sinusoidal, or irregular channels
- Endothelial lining only when directly resolved
- Perivascular cellular arrangement
- Congestion, hemorrhage, extravasated erythrocytes, thrombus-like material, and fibrin-like material

Rules:
1. Apply Global_Rules.md.
2. Distinguish true vascular channels from lumina, tissue clefts, retraction, and processing spaces when possible.
3. Do not infer endothelial lineage or vascular tumour type from channel morphology alone.
4. Use `vascular-like channel` when a lining cannot be confirmed.
5. Do not infer circulation or flow beyond static visible evidence.

Output:
- Vessels_or_Vascular_Like_Channels
- Vessel_Density
- Vessel_Caliber
- Vessel_Wall_Morphology
- Branching_or_Channel_Pattern
- Perivascular_Arrangement
- Congestion
- Hemorrhage
- Extravasated_Erythrocytes
- Thrombus_Like_Material
- Fibrin_Like_Material
- Assessability
- Limitations
